The Finish Menu

Six ways to turn concrete into the best surface in the building

Every system is chosen for how the space works — traffic, chemicals, moisture, and the look you want when the light hits it.

01 Signature Finish

Metallic Epoxy

Metallic pigments moved through the resin by hand — every pour swirls differently, and no two floors on earth match. Showrooms, lobbies, and garages that need to stop people mid-step start here.

02

Polished Concrete

The slab itself, ground and honed to a stone-like sheen. No coating to wear off — just concrete at its best.

03

Epoxy Floors

Seamless, chemical-resistant, easy to clean. The workhorse coating for shops, plants, and garages.

04

Flake Systems

Vinyl flake broadcast into the coating for texture, grip, and a finish that hides dust and drops.

05

Quartz Systems

Colored quartz aggregate locked in resin — heavy-duty slip resistance for wet and hard-working areas.

06

Self-Leveling

Pours that find their own level — flattening worn, wavy, or damaged slabs into a dead-flat new surface.

The Process

From bare slab to showpiece

  1. 1

    Walk the floor

    We look at the slab, the traffic it takes, and what you want it to become — then match the right system to it.

  2. 2

    Grind & prep

    Diamond grinding opens the concrete and fixes cracks and spalls. Prep is where floors are won or lost.

  3. 3

    Pour & finish

    Coats, broadcasts, or polishing passes — built up layer by layer to the finish you picked.

  4. 4

    Seal & hand over

    A final protective topcoat, a cure window, and a floor that's ready to work — and to show off.

Why Coated & Polished Floors

What the right floor system buys you

Takes a beating

Resinous and polished systems stand up to impacts, abrasion, hot tires, and heavy equipment.

Seamless & sealed

No joints or grout to trap oil, water, or grime — spills sit on top and wipe away.

Fast to maintain

Dust-proofed and easy to clean — a mop and a dust broom replace constant scrubbing and patching.

Looks like glass

From satin stone to mirror gloss to swirling metallics — the floor becomes the feature.
